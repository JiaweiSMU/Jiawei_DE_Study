### 1. Volume
**Scale of data** that is being generated daily, and is one of the characteristics of Big Data
### 2. Velocity
Rate at which data is being created, collected and processed. As there is a need for near real-time / real time processing methods
### 3. Variety
Diverse range of data types (`Structured Data, Unstructured Data`)
- Structured: Names, Phone Numbers etc.
- Unstructured: Images, Audio etc.
